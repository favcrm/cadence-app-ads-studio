# Ad Copy & Creative Policy Rubric

Check every drafted Google Ads Responsive Search Ad (RSA) or Meta Feed/Reels brief against these rules before attaching to a campaign:

1. **Exact commercial facts:** Preserve exact prices, currency (`HK$`), minimum quantities, and eligibility terms from the source brief. Never round, invent discounts, or add unverified "Free" claims.
2. **No unverified superlatives:** Avoid "best", "#1", "guaranteed", or health/financial outcome claims unless verified by an approved workspace source.
3. **Google RSA character caps:**
   - Headlines `H1`–`H3`: `<= 30` characters each (half-width count; wide CJK characters count as 2 in Google Ads).
   - Descriptions `D1`–`D2`: `<= 90` characters each.
4. **Media pointers:** Keep image/video binaries in Workspace Files (`global/ads-studio/assets/`); obey the `15 MiB` hosted upload cap (`WIKI_UPLOAD_MAX`) or link an approved external video ID for longer cuts.
5. **Spend discipline:** Drafts and campaign launches default to `PAUSED` until explicitly approved by the workspace owner.
